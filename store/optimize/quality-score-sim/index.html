◆ What Quality Score Affects

  • $
    Your CPC: Lower Quality Score = higher cost per click. A QS of 3 means you pay roughly 2x more than a competitor with QS 7 for the same position.
  • ↑
    Your Ad Rank: Ad Rank = Max Bid x Quality Score. A higher QS lets you outrank competitors without increasing your bid.
  • 👀
    Whether your ad shows: Google has minimum Quality Score thresholds. Very low QS (1-3) may prevent your ad from showing at all for competitive keywords.
